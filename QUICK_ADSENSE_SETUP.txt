═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  TROUBLESHOOTING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ Ads not showing?
   □ Check Publisher ID is correct (all 3 files)
   □ Check Ad Slot IDs are correct (2 files)
   □ Make sure NODE_ENV=production
   □ Disable browser ad-blocker
   □ Wait 24 hours after setup
   □ Check AdSense account is approved

❌ Console errors?
   □ Open DevTools → Console
   □ Look for "adsbygoogle" errors
   □ Verify IDs match AdSense dashboard

❌ Layout issues?
   □ Ads should not overlap content
   □ Check responsive design on mobile
   □ Test different screen sizes
